## Design Context

### Users
Prospective researchers, students, partners, funders, policy makers, and people participating in AARI surveys. They need a clear first understanding of the institute and easy paths into its programs, publications, and research participation.

### Brand Personality
Independent, industry-leading, bold, African.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light, refined minimal, bold data-led, and warm. The site should feel like an editorial research institution rather than a generic nonprofit template.

### Design Principles
- Make the breadth of AARI's research legible at a glance.
- Pair confident typography with generous, calm white space.
- Use data and evidence as visual structure, not decoration.
- Keep participation and research discovery one click away.
- Carry an African point of view without reducing it to visual clichés.
